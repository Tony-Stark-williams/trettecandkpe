History of National Cleaning in Uganda

National cleaning in Uganda started in 2026 when the Prime Minister called for the last Saturday of every month to be dedicated to cleaning public spaces. Since that declaration, communities across the country have embraced the practice.

Health, environmental and economic benefits

  • Reduces disease and improves public health.
  • Attracts visitors and investors, boosting the local economy.
  • Supports safe food production by keeping environments clean and hygienic.
