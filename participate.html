HOW TO PARTICIPATE

Everyone can join the Clean Uganda Initiative. By taking part in regular cleanups, creating awareness, and working together, we can keep our communities healthy, clean, and beautiful.

Ways to participate

  • Join neighborhood cleanup activities every last Saturday of the month.
  • Volunteer in community cleanups, sweeping, slashing grass, and collecting litter.
  • Report areas that need cleaning or waste collection.
  • Encourage your family, friends, and neighbors to take part.
  • Support the initiative by sharing ideas and helping organize events.

What you need to do

  1. Visit the contact page and register your interest.
  2. Choose the type of cleaning activity you want to take part in.
  3. Join the scheduled community cleanup day.
  4. Bring your work gloves, protective gear, and a willingness to help.
  5. Work together and keep the environment clean.
