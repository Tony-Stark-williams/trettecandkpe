How to participate

Everyone can join the Clean Uganda Initiative. Here are practical ways to get involved:

Ways to help

  • Join neighborhood cleanups every last Saturday of the month.
  • Volunteer for sweeping, collecting litter, or slashing grass.
  • Organize a local cleanup and invite your neighbors.
  • Report areas that need attention so local teams can respond.

What to bring

  1. Protective gloves and boots.
  2. Tools like rakes, brooms, and bags.
  3. Water, sunscreen, and a positive attitude.
