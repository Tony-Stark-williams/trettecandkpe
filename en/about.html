HISTORY OF NATIONAL CLEANING IN UGANDA

National cleaning in uganda was staretd in 2026 under the orders of the prime minister who declared to the country that every last saturday of the month should be dedicated for cleaning the country and then rendered as a public holiday

Since then the culture has been adopted by the citizens and now they particiapte with all their hearts to see that they promote a healthy environment in their country which has helped to improve their health conditions

HEALTH ENVIRONMENTAL AND ECONOMIC BENEFITS OF A CLEAN COUNTRY

  • A clean country enables the citizens to live a hygiene-disease free life
  • A clean country helps the country to attract different people from different areas who might as well as come as investors boosting the country's economy
  • A clean country supports the establishment of food processing industries as the area is clean so the foods produced do no get contaminated
