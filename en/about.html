HISTORY OF NATIONAL CLEANING IN UGANDA

National cleaning in Uganda started in 2026 under the orders of the Prime Minister, who declared that every last Saturday of the month should be dedicated to cleaning the country.

Since then, the culture has been adopted by citizens, who participate with great commitment to promote a healthy environment and improve the beauty of their country.

HEALTH, ENVIRONMENTAL AND ECONOMIC BENEFITS OF A CLEAN COUNTRY

  • A clean country enables citizens to live a healthy, hygiene-conscious life.
  • A clean country attracts investors and visitors, boosting the economy.
  • A clean country supports food processing industries and reduces contamination risks.
